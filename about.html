About

Raquel Ledo Doval is a biotechnologist and marine molecular biologist working across marine biotechnology, fungal biology, microbiology and interdisciplinary research.

Her doctoral research at Utrecht University and the Westerdijk Fungal Biodiversity Institute focused on marine fungi, their physiology and extracellular enzyme systems, and their responses to seaweed and other complex biomasses. Her scientific background spans microbial cultivation, molecular biology, advanced microscopy, fermentation and bioprocessing, physiological and enzymatic screening, and biochemical characterisation of biological materials.

Her research interests centre on marine and aquatic microorganisms, seaweeds, blue biotechnology and biological approaches to the use and processing of marine resources. Alongside experimental research, she develops and coordinates collaborative projects connecting science with art, design, technology and society.

Her work includes European art–science programmes, marine expeditions, artist–scientist collaborations, bioart and biodesign initiatives, workshops and community-facing research. Working across scientific, cultural and applied contexts, she is particularly interested in projects that connect marine knowledge with collaborative forms of research.

She welcomes research collaborations, project-development and coordination roles, and advisory work across marine biotechnology, blue bioeconomy and interdisciplinary practice.
