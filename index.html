Practice overview

My practice moves between experimental biology and collaborative research. It is grounded in marine fungi, seaweeds and microbial systems, and extends into microscopy, bioprocessing, biological materials, project development and interdisciplinary methods. I am interested in how scientific knowledge can operate across laboratory, environmental, cultural and applied contexts.

Marine & microbial biotechnology

Marine fungi, seaweeds, microbial physiology, enzymes, biomass and blue biotechnology.

Molecular, imaging & bioprocess methods

Molecular biology, microscopy, cultivation, fermentation and biochemical analysis.

Interdisciplinary & collaborative research

Art–science, biodesign, biological materials, sound, workshops, European projects and knowledge exchange.
