S+T+ARTS Aqua Motion / Hortimare

My role: Project Coordinator · 2024–present

Coordination of artist–scientist residencies connecting aquatic innovation with microscopy, biomaterials, seaweed-based research and cosmetics. The project brings together interdisciplinary partners to develop collaborative research and public-facing outputs.

Marine biotechnology · low-trophic aquaculture

From Sea to Society

My role: Researcher / project developer · 2024–present

Research connecting marine fungi, seaweed biomass and low-trophic aquaculture, including fungal physiology, extracellular enzyme systems and access to valuable components of seaweed biomass.

Ocean · sound · transdisciplinary research

OCEAN/UNI — Sound Ecologies

My role: Collaborator / workshop co-developer · 2024–present

Collaborative research and workshop development connecting ocean ecologies, listening, sound and interdisciplinary knowledge production. The project includes concrete outputs such as Diffractive Ensembles: Silence as a Thread for Listening.

Art–science · biological imaging

STUDIOTOPIA / GLUON

My role: Expert Scientist · 2024–present

Artist–scientist collaboration using microscopy and biological research as shared methods for investigating microbial and ecological processes. The project focuses on how laboratory methods can support collaborative inquiry in creative settings.
